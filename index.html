I was a researcher at Alibaba Cloud and a Postdoctoral Researcher at University of Oxford. I obtained my DPhil in Engineering Science from Oxford, advised by Prof. Noa Zilberman. I had the pleasure of working with Prof. Leandros Tassiulas during a research visit at Yale University. I received dual BEng degrees from the University of Glasgow (first-class honours) and UESTC.

My research interests include data center networks, AI computing networks, and space networks. I am particularly interested in leveraging in-network computing and machine learning techniques to design efficient and scalable network systems and address fundamental challenges in networking.
